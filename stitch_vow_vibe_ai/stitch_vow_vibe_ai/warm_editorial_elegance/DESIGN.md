## Brand & Style

This design system mediates between high-touch celebratory romance and sophisticated, data-dense event logistics. It targets discerning couples, high-end event planners, and creative vendors who demand both emotional inspiration and surgical project precision.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Warmth**:
- High-contrast, literary typographic hierarchy evoking bespoke wedding stationery and luxury print publications.
- Generous breathing room, precise hairline architectural divisions, and structured grid arrangements that prevent cognitive overload across complex timelines, budgets, and seating layouts.
- Subtle warmth in light modes that sidesteps sterile corporate SaaS tropes, replacing clinical cool grays with warm ivory, rich botanical plum tones, and antique metallic champagne highlights.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-touch celebration in deep, confident authority while keeping workspaces luminous and readable.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#35213D` - Deep Plum):** The anchor for brand presence, primary actions, heavy typography, and prominent active navigation. Provides dramatic grounding contrast without the cold sterility of pure black.
- **Secondary (`#D9B77E` - Champagne Gold):** Reserved for accent states, luxury signifiers, AI-generation badges, active celebratory milestones, and elevated borders.
- **Tertiary (`#E9A1B0` - Rose Pink):** An expressive flourish color for moodboard tags, romantic cues, soft progress fills, and selected interactive highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FFF9F4` - Warm Ivory):** The primary backdrop across all views. Creates an organic, tactile paper-like foundation.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for foreground containers, cards, floating modal dialogs, and workspace worktops.
- **Surface Tint (`#F0EAF6` - Soft Lavender):** Used for subtle secondary container fills, selected row backgrounds, and soft tag fills.

### Text & Feedback Tokens
- **Text Primary (`#25212A`):** Deep plum-tinted charcoal for high-contrast, effortless legibility.
- **Text Secondary (`#756D79`):** Subdued plum-gray for metadata, labels, and timestamps.
- **Success (`#27856A`):** Deep botanical emerald for completed tasks, budget approvals, and confirmed RSVPs.
- **Warning (`#D99A32`):** Ochre amber for payment milestones due and pending vendor contracts.
- **Error (`#C74B58`):** Crimson berry for overdue deadlines, over-budget alerts, and form validation errors.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the editorial grace of **Playfair Display** with the clear, high-density utilitarian readability of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Usage Guidelines
- **Playfair Display:** Exclusively applied to milestone metrics, page titles, section introductions, AI concept headings, and editorial content cards. Never use Playfair Display below 20px, as fine serifs degrade readability in low-scale dashboard environments.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Applied to interactive controls, operational data, tables, guest rosters, timeline task items, forms, and general interface copy.
- **Capitalization & Letter Spacing:** Use uppercase treatment only for `label-sm` (e.g., status pill trackers, table headers, currency indicators) combined with wide letter spacing (+0.04em) to maintain crisp, quiet legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adheres to a flexible 12-column responsive grid anchored by generous outer margins that preserve an editorial, publication-like cadence.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 32px (`2rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Dual-pane layouts balance editorial inspiration (moodboards, AI narrative prompts) on the left with structured operational tables (budgets, schedules) on the right.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) page margins. Side panels collapse into progressive overlay drawers.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) page margins. Complex interactive grids (e.g., seating charts and timeline builders) transform into tabbed or horizontal scroll-snagged workflows.

### Spacing Discipline
Internal component paddings rely on `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px) to retain an airy, high-end feel. Avoid dense, cramped layouts; event planning involves emotional weight and requires visual clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through **Tonal Layering** paired with **Low-Contrast Hairline Outlines** and warm ambient shadows.

### Elevation Tiers
1. **Canvas (Base Level):** Solid `#FFF9F4` (Warm Ivory). Default view background.
2. **Surface Level 1 (Card & Section Workspaces):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid hairline border colored `#35213D` at 8% opacity (`rgba(51, 33, 61, 0.08)`).
3. **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements, Dropdowns, Popovers):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) supported by a warm ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(51, 33, 61, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(51, 33, 61, 0.04)`.
4. **Surface Level 3 (Dialogs, Modal Takeovers):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a deeper plum-tinted drop shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(51, 33, 61, 0.16)`. Backdrop overlay uses `#35213D` with 30% opacity combined with an 8px blur filter to keep focus calm and theatrical.

## Shapes

The design language uses controlled softness (`roundedness: 1` — 4px base radius) to retain an architectural, tailored appearance reminiscent of fine paper stationery and invitations.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Table Rows):** `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, crisp corners with gentle tactile easing.
- **Medium Components (Buttons, Dropdown Menus, Toast Notifications):** `0.375rem` (6px).
- **Cards, Containers, Modal Panes (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, conversational AI prompt chips, and avatar containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Deep Plum (`#35213D`), text in Warm Ivory (`#FFF9F4`). Subdued hover state with 90% opacity and a smooth 150ms transition.
- **Secondary / Champagne:** Outline in Champagne Gold (`#D9B77E`) with text in Deep Plum (`#35213D`). Hover applies a 10% Champagne Gold background fill.
- **AI Action Variant:** Deep Plum background featuring an inset 1px Champagne Gold border and gold star icon, signifying automated generation or optimization.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, Plum text, subtle background hover using Soft Lavender (`#F0EAF6`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Compact height (24px), full pill radius, `label-sm` typography. 
  - *Confirmed/Success:* Soft emerald tint background with `#27856A` text.
  - *Pending/Action Needed:* Soft amber tint with `#D99A32` text.
  - *AI Recommendation:* `#F0EAF6` (Soft Lavender) background with `#35213D` text and `#D9B77E` active edge.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Pure White background, 1px hairline border in `rgba(51, 33, 61, 0.15)`. Height of 42px with 12px horizontal padding.
- Focused state transitions border to Deep Plum (`#35213D`) with a 2px outer aura in `#D9B77E` at 30% opacity. Never use harsh browser-default rings.
- Placeholder text styled with Secondary Text (`#756D79`) at 60% opacity.

### Cards & Moodboard Panels
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 8px corner radius, hairline border in `rgba(51, 33, 61, 0.08)`.
- Cards balance editorial photography headers with organized metadata tables underneath. Card titles use `headline-sm` (Playfair Display) to preserve visual elegance.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 18px dimensions, 4px corner radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Unchecked state: 1.5px border in `rgba(51, 33, 61, 0.3)`.
- Checked state: Solid `#35213D` fill with `#FFF9F4` checkmark icon.

### Specialized AI & Event Modules
- **AI Prompt Console:** A docked Warm Ivory or White container with a champagne accent border, allowing natural language queries (e.g., *"Rebalance seating chart for 8 cancellations"*).
- **Interactive Timeline Nodes:** Thin vertical hairline tracks connecting plum circular milestone markers, accented with gold sparkles for critical deadlines.
